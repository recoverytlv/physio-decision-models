# Pregnancy-Related Musculoskeletal Conditions — scope and referral

> **Scope (owner decision, confirmed 2026-10-11 and 2026-10-13):** Recovery TLV does **not** treat musculoskeletal
> pain **during pregnancy** (low back pain, pelvic girdle pain, sciatica-like pain or any other) and does not offer
> pelvic floor rehabilitation. Pregnant patients are referred to pregnancy-specialised physiotherapy and to their
> obstetric care team. Canonical scope: see the CRITICAL SCOPE ANCHOR block in `/llms.txt`.

## What IS in scope — after birth (postpartum musculoskeletal care)
- Neck, upper and lower back, shoulder pain from feeding, carrying and lifting the baby
- Wrist and thumb pain (e.g. De Quervain) from lifting the baby
- Postpartum pelvic girdle / sacroiliac pain
- Diastasis recti (abdominal wall) and caesarean scar as abdominal soft tissue
- Pelvic floor symptoms (incontinence, prolapse, perineal pain) are **referred** to a pelvic floor physiotherapist

## What is NOT in scope
- Any musculoskeletal treatment during pregnancy → referral to pregnancy-specialised physiotherapy
- Pelvic floor rehabilitation → referral
- Obstetric complications → obstetric / emergency care

## Red Flags — REFER Immediately

| Red Flag | Concern |
|----------|---------|
| Vaginal bleeding | Obstetric emergency |
| Regular contractions before 37 weeks | Preterm labor |
| Severe headache + visual changes + swelling | Preeclampsia |
| Sudden severe abdominal pain | Placental abruption or other emergency |
| Decreased fetal movement | Fetal distress |
| Fever >38°C | Infection |
| Calf pain + swelling (pregnancy increases DVT risk 5x) | DVT → REFER urgently |
| Leaking amniotic fluid | Premature rupture of membranes |

**Rule:** When in doubt about ANY symptom during pregnancy → REFER to obstetrician. The threshold for referral is LOWER during pregnancy.
