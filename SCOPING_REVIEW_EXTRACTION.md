## Preliminary observations (NOT synthesised findings — for protocol refinement)

- **Dominant outcome instruments:** VISA-A (Achilles), VISA-P (patellar), SPADI/DASH
  (rotator cuff), PFGS/PRTEE (lateral elbow) — supports the framework's region metrics.
- **A concrete "meaningful change" anchor exists:** VISA-A MCID ≈ 10 (Pringels 2025).
- **Progression/decision criteria are predominantly pain-based and lack consensus**
  (Escriche-Escuder 2020; Ortega-Castillo 2022) → this is the literature gap the
  framework addresses.
- **Emerging criteria-based / performance-based decisions** (Griffin 2021; Corrigan
  2022) point beyond pain toward strength, reactive strength and jump performance.
- **Real-world non-response is measurable:** ~24% unrecovered at 5 years despite PT
  (Deng 2025); prior treatment failure common (Breda 2021).
- **A recurring escalation path:** conservative first → modify/escalate → refer/surgery
  when it fails (von Rickenbach 2021; Tarantino 2023; Aicale 2020).

## Preliminary observations — batch 2 (NOT synthesised findings)

- **Imaging/structure is a poor single predictor** of who will respond (Breda 2022;
  Zwerver 2023) → response must be judged by *function and load capacity*, not scans.
- **Dose matters and is under-reported:** intensity (external load) drives effect;
  volume inconsistent (Pavlova 2023) → strengthens the treatment-fidelity gate.
- **Comparative map for the "modify/escalate" step:** loading is first-line; adjuncts
  (ESWT/LLLT moderate; PRP inconclusive; corticosteroids short-term only, harmful
  long-term) (Irby 2020) — relevant later for a treatment-options document.
- **Chronicity and slow recovery are the norm** (Cardoso 2019) → reframes "no quick
  improvement" away from "failure."
- **Honest treatment uncertainty** (Lowdon 2024) reinforces deciding by *response*,
  not by *treatment label*.
