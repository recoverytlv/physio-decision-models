# Scoping Review Protocol (PRISMA-ScR)

> Draft protocol. Reported per PRISMA-ScR. Methodology follows the JBI scoping
> review framework. Registration (OSF) to be completed before screening.

## 1. Title

When Should Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy Be Continued, Modified, Referred or
Discontinued? A Scoping Review and Proposed Decision Framework.

## 2. Rationale

Decisions to continue or stop MSK physiotherapy are often driven by subjective
report and clinician persistence rather than explicit criteria. Evidence on
discharge criteria, early-response prediction, treatment fidelity, and
non-response is scattered and not consolidated into a decision framework. A
scoping review is appropriate to map a heterogeneous, emerging body of literature.

## 3. Research question

What criteria, frameworks, and decision points guide the continuation,
modification, referral, or discontinuation of MSK physiotherapy when treatment
produces no measurable improvement (non-response)?

## 4. PCC eligibility framework

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Population** | Adults (and adolescent athletes) receiving outpatient MSK physiotherapy |
| **Concept** | Non-response / treatment failure; discharge & continuation criteria; time-limited trials; early-response prediction; referral criteria; decision frameworks |
| **Context** | Outpatient/clinical MSK physiotherapy; conservative and post-operative; any country |

## 5. Inclusion criteria

- Peer-reviewed studies, guidelines, consensus statements, and reviews
- Address criteria/decisions for continuing, modifying, referring, or stopping MSK physiotherapy
- Define/measure treatment response, non-response, discharge, or referral
- English language; 2000–2026

## 6. Exclusion criteria

- Non-MSK conditions (neurological, cardiorespiratory, paediatric developmental)
- Surgical decision-making without a physiotherapy decision component
- Opinion pieces without explicit criteria
- Conference abstracts without retrievable full text

## 7. Information sources

PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase (if accessible), CINAHL, PEDro, Cochrane Library, and
Google Scholar (grey literature / guidelines). Hand-search reference lists of
included reviews.

## 8. Search strategy (reproducible — PubMed seed)

```
(physiotherapy OR "physical therapy" OR rehabilitation)
AND ("discharge criteria" OR "treatment discontinuation" OR "stopping rules"
     OR "non-response" OR "treatment failure" OR "early response"
     OR "rate of recovery" OR "exit criteria" OR "when to stop" OR cessation
     OR "referral criteria" OR "clinical prediction")
AND (musculoskeletal OR orthopaedic OR orthopedic OR "low back pain"
     OR tendinopathy OR shoulder OR knee OR hip OR neck)
Filters: 2000–2026; English.
```
Search to be adapted per database; full strings + dates recorded in the review.

## 9. Selection process

Two-stage screening (title/abstract → full text). Where feasible, dual screening
with conflict resolution; otherwise single screening with a verified sample
re-check. Reasons for full-text exclusion recorded. Flow reported in a PRISMA-ScR
diagram.

## 10. Data extraction (charting) fields

| Field | Captures |
|---|---|
| Citation | Author, year, journal, DOI/PMID |
| Design | Study/document type |
| Population/condition | MSK region, conservative/post-op |
| Definition of (non-)response | How it is operationalised |
| Outcome/measure | Instrument + threshold (MCID/MDC) |
| Decision window | Sessions / time to decision |
| Decision criteria | Continue / modify / refer / stop |
| Framework/rule | Any named model or algorithm |
| Gaps noted | Author-reported limitations |

## 11. Synthesis

Narrative + tabular synthesis mapping definitions, criteria, decision windows,
and frameworks; identification of gaps. Outputs feed the **proposed MSK
Physiotherapy Non-Response Decision Framework** (see NON_RESPONSE_FRAMEWORK.md).

## 12. Integrity

Every included reference verified (no fabricated citations). AI assistance
disclosed. Author (A. Zubrisky) is accountable for the final content.
